Excellence Award: See You at States!

12790X Entropic Alpacas won the Excellence Award at our November 15 competition! The award recognizes the team’s work across robot design, engineering documentation, programming, match performance, and teamwork.

This win qualifies us for the state championship. We’re proud of how far the robot and team have come this season—and we’re ready to keep improving.

We’ll see you at states!
